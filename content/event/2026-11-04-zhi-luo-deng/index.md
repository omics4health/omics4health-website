This session will focus on **Metax enables accurate cross-domain taxonomic profiling of metagenomes**, a study published in *Cell*. The paper title is used here as the session topic.

本期报告将围绕这篇 *Cell* 论文展开，目前以论文题目作为活动主题。

## Speaker / 报告人

**Zhi-Luo Deng, PhD**  
Helmholtz Centre for Infection Research, Germany

His work focuses on bioinformatics and the human microbiome, including metagenomic and metatranscriptomic analysis. He is the first author of the featured Metax study.

[Speaker CV / 讲者简历](https://dawnmy.github.io/CV/)

## Featured paper / 本期论文

Zhi-Luo Deng, Nasim Safaei and Alice Carolyn McHardy. **Metax enables accurate cross-domain taxonomic profiling of metagenomes.** *Cell* (2026). DOI: **10.1016/j.cell.2026.08.024**.

Metax uses the distribution of sequencing reads across reference genomes to help distinguish genuine microbial signals from false positives and improve estimates of community composition. This session will center on the study and its approach to metagenomic analysis.

Metax 利用测序序列在参考基因组上的覆盖分布，帮助区分真实微生物信号与假阳性，提高物种鉴定及丰度估计的准确性。本期将围绕这一研究及其宏基因组分析方法展开交流。
